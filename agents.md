Core idea

Projects enter time-limited public competitions called Arenas.



Builders can pay to enter selected Arenas.



Visitors discover competing projects, visit them, support them, and watch live rankings change.



The purpose of Project Arena is to help projects gain:



qualified clicks

discovery

traction

attention

social proof

reputation

repeat exposure



Project Arena should feel like a combination of:



internet discovery + competitive events + live leaderboards + project promotion



It should NOT feel like a conventional startup directory.

POSITIONING

Primary positioning:



PROJECT ARENA



Where projects compete for attention.



Alternative supporting language:



Discover. Compete. Get seen.



The internet is building. See what's winning.



The product should eventually become a place people visit even when they are not promoting anything because the competitions themselves are entertaining and useful for discovering interesting projects.

CORE TERMINOLOGY

Use these terms consistently.



Project
The product, app, game, tool, community, repository, website, or other internet project being promoted.



Builder
The person or team behind a project.



Arena
A competitive event.



Supporter
A visitor supporting a project.



Champion
The winner of an Arena.



Arena Rating
A project's long-term competitive reputation/rating.



Arena Entry
A project's participation in an Arena.



Avoid referring to every participant as a startup or founder.

PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

Project Arena should be:



competitive

entertaining

transparent

fast

visually distinctive

easy to understand

highly shareable

useful for project discovery

capable of generating measurable traffic for participants



The platform should create a flywheel:



Projects enter an Arena.



↓



Projects share their participation/ranking.



↓



Their audiences visit Project Arena.



↓



Visitors discover other projects.



↓



Projects receive clicks and supporters.



↓



Participants see measurable value.



↓



More projects enter future Arenas.

IMPORTANT BUSINESS PRINCIPLE

Do not reduce Project Arena to:



Pay money → receive higher ranking.



Money should primarily buy:



Arena entry

campaign participation

premium event formats

additional exposure opportunities

analytics

optional sponsorships



Projects should generally have to earn rankings through competition.



This makes Project Arena more credible and more entertaining than a simple paid leaderboard.



RESPONSE STYLE

Be direct and concrete.



Prefer:



Build this because X.



over vague recommendations.



Use diagrams, flows, tables, schemas, or wireframes when they improve clarity.



When giving multiple possibilities, rank them.



When one option is clearly strongest, recommend it.



Do not repeatedly explain what Project Arena is unless the explanation is necessary.



Treat Project Arena as an ongoing product being actively designed and built.
